# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in octoscode respectful, inclusive,
and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of experience, identity,
background, appearance, ability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints offered in good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect private information and consider the safety of users and contributors.
- Respect project decisions even when you disagree; use the documented
  governance process to appeal or propose a change.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or attention, unwanted contact, or deliberate outing.
- Trolling, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an
  investigation.
- Any conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project in public or private community spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not put sensitive conduct details in a public issue. Open a
[conduct contact request](https://github.com/octos-org/octoscode/issues/new?template=conduct_contact.yml)
only to ask for a private channel; do not identify people or describe the
incident in that public request.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, preserve confidentiality as far as
practical, and avoid conflicts of interest. Depending on severity and history,
responses may include a private correction, warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Immediate
action may be taken when safety or project integrity is at risk. A person named
in a report will not be the sole decision maker for that report.

Reports made in good faith will not lead to retaliation. Knowingly false or
malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.
